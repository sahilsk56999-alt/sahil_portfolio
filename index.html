A practice built at the seam between architecture and code.

STRATA was founded on a simple observation: the buildings and interfaces we admire most are rarely decorated well — they're structured well. We work as one team across architecture, environmental engineering, and software, so that structural logic and digital logic are never designed apart.

Every project starts as a system diagram, not a rendering. The form follows once the logic holds.
